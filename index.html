The Mint

A tool to calculate and compare cryptographic hashes (e.g., SHA256, MD5) of files for integrity verification. A struck coin proves itself against the die; a file proves itself against its hash, and two hashes either match in every line or they do not.

What it does

The tool calculates and compares cryptographic hashes (e.g., SHA256, MD5) of files, for integrity verification. Usage, as the README gives it: ./analyze-file-hasher [params]. Its one requirement is pandas>=2.1.0.
